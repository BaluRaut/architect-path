# ADR-000: Title as a decision, not a topic

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-00X
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:**

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the load, the deadline, the thing that broke.
Written so someone who was not there can follow it in a year.

## Options considered

For each: what it is, and the single reason it was or was not chosen. Two or three
options. If you only had one, you were not deciding, you were justifying.

## Decision

One paragraph, in the active voice. "We will…"

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard. The second half is the part people skip
and the part that matters, because it is what the next person inherits.

## What would change our mind

The measurement or event that should make someone reopen this. An ADR with no
falsification condition is a preference written down formally.
